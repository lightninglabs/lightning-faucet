## 📝 About Your LNO1 Offer

The string you showed me:
```
lno1qgsyxjtl6luzd9t3pr62xr7eemp6awnejusgf6gw45q75vcfqqqqqqq2qq...
```

This is a **BOLT12 Lightning Offer** - it's like a reusable payment address!

**What's the difference?**

| Type | Format | Usage | Support |
|------|--------|-------|---------|
| **Regular Invoice** | `lntb...` | One-time use | ✅ Works everywhere |
| **BOLT12 Offer** | `lno1...` | Reusable | ⚠️ Limited support (newer tech) |

**Why not use the offer?**
- Voltage might not support BOLT12 offers yet
- Not all Lightning wallets support it
- Regular invoices (`lntb...`) work everywhere

**When to use the offer?**
- Share it with people who have BOLT12-compatible wallets
- They can fetch invoices from it and pay you multiple times
- It's like your "Lightning payment address"
