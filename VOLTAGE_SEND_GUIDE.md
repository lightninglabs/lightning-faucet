## 📱 Where to Find Send/Pay in Voltage Dashboard

### Option 1: Top Navigation
Look at the top of your Voltage dashboard for:
- **"Send"** button
- **"Pay"** button  
- **"Lightning"** section
- Usually near "Receive" or "Balance"

### Option 2: Left Sidebar
Click on **"Transactions"** in the left menu, then look for:
- **"Send Transaction"**
- **"Pay Invoice"**
- **"Lightning Payment"**

### Option 3: Main Dashboard
Look for cards or sections that say:
- **"Send"**
- **"Pay Invoice"**
- **"Lightning"**
- Usually near your balance card

### Option 4: Applications
Try clicking on **"LNBits"** (it shows "Waiting Unlock"):
- LNBits often has send/pay functionality
- You might need to unlock it first

### Option 5: Look for These Buttons
Search for any button that says:
- **"Pay"**
- **"Send"**
- **"Lightning"**
- **"Invoice"**
- **"+"** (might open more options)
